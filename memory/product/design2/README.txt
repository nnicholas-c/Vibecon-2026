Drivepast opening kit (Saturday Oct 24, 2026)

What this is
- PROMPT.md is the message for Emergent. It replaces the earlier design update.
- data/ holds the city model and the demo routes as JSON. It's data only, with no code, so it stays within the hackathon rule against outside code.

How to send it
1. In the running Emergent project, attach this zip. If Emergent won't take a zip, attach the nine files in data/ instead.
2. Paste all of PROMPT.md as the message.
3. Don't attach screenshots of other websites with it.

Where the data came from
- Buildings, roads and water come from OpenFreeMap vector tiles (OpenMapTiles schema, OpenStreetMap data), build of Oct 18, 2026.
- The shadows were merged from those buildings for the sun at 1:45pm on Oct 24 in Emeryville.
- The six demo routes were computed with the public OSRM server through made-up stops. Bayline Courier and the Emeryville lab are fictional.
